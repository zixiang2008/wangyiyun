## Reddit / XDA / Forum opening paragraph (English)

Hi all, I’m organizing a public rescue/reverse-engineering effort for an abandoned Netease Sing Cloud Speaker (`网易三音云音箱`).

The original ecosystem appears to have degraded to the point that the device is no longer easy to reconfigure or use. It has no 3.5mm input, does not currently behave like a normal Bluetooth speaker for me, and yet the hardware appears worth saving.

Public teardown clues suggest `Allwinner R16-J`, `AP6236`, `BCM43436`, `Mini-USB`, `Reset`, and a reserved `TF` slot. I put the known information, evidence checklist, collaboration templates, and probe script in a public repo:

https://github.com/zixiang2008/wangyiyun

I’d especially appreciate help from people experienced in BLE, embedded Linux/Android, UART/debug ports, firmware extraction, or speaker repurposing.
